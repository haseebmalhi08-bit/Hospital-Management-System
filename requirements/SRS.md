# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

# Introduction

The Hospital Management System manages patients,
doctors, admissions, treatments, and discharge records.

# Functional Requirements

## FR1
System shall register patients.

## FR2
System shall assign doctors.

## FR3
System shall admit patients.

## FR4
System shall discharge patients.

# Non Functional Requirements

## NFR1
System shall maintain consistency.

## NFR2
System shall provide secure access.
